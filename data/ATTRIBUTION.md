# Asset attribution

Vehicle and ground assets are from [Blade](https://github.com/kvark/blade)
`examples/vehicle/data` (MIT license):

- `ground.gltf` / `ground.bin`
- `raceFuture-body.glb`, `raceFuture.ron`
- `wheelRacing.glb`
- `orange_light_grid.png`
- `level.ron` (adapted layout; walls retained)

The following arena look assets are original to Screech and are not derived from
Twisted Metal 2 (or any other game) artwork. They are generated by
`python3 scripts/generate-tm2-look.py`:

- `tm2-sky.hdr` — equirectangular arcade sky (Radiance HDR)
- `arena-walls.gltf` — tall colored perimeter walls
- `landmark-tower.gltf` — low-poly monument tower
- `arena-plaza.gltf` / `arena-plaza.png` — warm asphalt plaza overlay
- `charge-pad.gltf` — see above / `scripts/generate-charge-pad.py`
